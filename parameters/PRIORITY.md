# Priority for user-supplied dataset ingestion

**P0 — first defensible mission slice**
1. Georeferenced Jezero DEM with metadata and NoData.
2. Orthorectified imagery co-registered to terrain.
3. Route origin, science target and a return path, clearly labeled hypothetical.
4. Explicit user-chosen astronaut and suit scenario (not NASA-certified constants).

**P1 — independent scientific context**
5. Perseverance MEDA archival Jezero atmosphere with sol/time and quality flags.
6. Additional mission data, e.g. MOLA topography, imaging products, or geology.
7. Curiosity RAD as *Gale historical reference only* with uncertainty and model transfer caveats.

**P2 — research extension**
8. Thermal / spectral / subsurface geological products.
9. More detailed suit, heat, mobility, atmospheric and probabilistic model calibration.

Declare source products only after reading their official labels, and separately check whether a chosen source is on the Space Apps challenge Resources tab.
