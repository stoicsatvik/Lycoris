# Candidate scientific data products

**Status:** discovery leads only. Nothing listed here has been ingested into Lycoris or confirmed to satisfy the challenge Resources-tab eligibility rule.

| Candidate | Measurement | Official lead | Initial status |
|---|---|---|---|
| NASA JPL Mars Trek | Planetary image layers / map context | https://trek.nasa.gov/ | Candidate; imagery is not a numerical DEM |
| USGS Mars 2020 science-investigation CTX DEM mosaic | Jezero terrain elevation | https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_2020_science_investigation_ctx_dem_mosaic | Candidate; product and CRS validation required |
| USGS Mars 2020 TRN CTX DTM mosaic | Jezero terrain elevation, different product | https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_2020_terrain_relative_navigation_ctx_dtm_mosaic | Candidate; do not interchange pixel grids with science-investigation mosaic |
| MGS MOLA | Regional / global topography | https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mgs/mola.html | Candidate; too coarse for individual rocks |
| Perseverance MEDA | Historical Jezero meteorology | https://pds-atmospheres.nmsu.edu/data_and_services/atmospheres_data/PERSEVERANCE/meda.html | Candidate; not future forecast |
| Curiosity RAD | Historical Gale ionizing radiation observations | https://science.nasa.gov/resource/radiation-measurements-on-mars/ | Candidate; Gale observations are not Jezero observations |

Before using a source in a competition entry, independently verify that at least one primary dataset satisfies the current official challenge Resources-tab rules. Record complete attribution and licensing.
