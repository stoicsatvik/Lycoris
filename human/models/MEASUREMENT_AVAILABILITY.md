# What can be measured, and when

| Modality | Candidate information | Restriction |
|---|---|---|
| Wearable / surface sensors | Pulse, selected ECG, motion, skin temperature, sleep actigraphy | Signal quality, artifacts and calibration; not an organ-level clinical diagnosis |
| Breath analysis / metabolic cart | Oxygen uptake, carbon dioxide output, ventilation | Equipment-intensive; no automatic EVA availability |
| Blood / urine / saliva laboratory | Blood counts, endocrine assays, inflammation, electrolytes, renal markers | Requires specimens and assay infrastructure; often off-EVA |
| Medical imaging | Bone mineral density, MRI brain/heart, ocular OCT, organ morphology | Complex equipment; not continuous suit telemetry |
| Instrumented terrestrial exercise | Gait, balance, torque, power, exercise thresholds | Transfer from 1g/partial-g analogs requires validation |
| Validated cognitive tests | Vigilance, reaction time, memory, task switching | Task training and practice effects, context required |
| Voluntary, validated self-report | Sleepiness, mood, stress, pain, social support | Context-sensitive and confidential; no psychiatric diagnosis |
| Model-only / latent states | Tissue damage rates, detailed fatigue, cancer probabilities | May be non-identifiable from available data; abstain when unsupported |

No patient or real astronaut personal measurement data are included in this registry.

Each future observation will reference a person pseudonym and timepoint in an access-controlled store; raw health data must not be exposed by GitHub.
