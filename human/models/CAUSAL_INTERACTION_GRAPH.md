# Human subsystem causal/dependency map

**Physical drivers:**
- Gravity level, loading history and task mechanics -> gait/joint loading and muscle recruitment -> movement energetics, fatigue, injury mechanisms -> walking speed.
- Gravity transition -> vestibular adaptation and cardiovascular regulation -> postural stability, task performance and potential orthostatic symptoms.
- Activity intensity + nutrition + rest -> substrate availability and metabolic demand -> O2 consumption, CO2 production, heat generation and fatigue state.
- Mission schedule + lighting + noise + workload -> sleep and circadian timing -> alertness, reaction time and operational error *associations*.
- Radiation exposure + shielding/particle spectrum + tissue-specific biology -> absorbed/equivalent tissue doses -> long-horizon outcomes under explicitly validated dose-response models.
- Space environment and mission stressors -> immune, endocrine and microbial responses, strongly confounded by sleep/nutrition/exercise and individual health.
- Isolation + communication delays + team processes -> social stressors and psychological/cognitive performance, highly person-dependent.

Causal arrows are **candidate science mechanisms**, not proven coefficients. Do not infer psychiatric diagnoses or individual pathology from sparse proxy data.

## Time state
Dependencies with reciprocal effects are evaluated using a time-dependent state-transition model, not a computationally cyclic directed acyclic graph.

## Coupling to other Lycoris components
Only expose versioned, measured-or-supported outputs. No direct transfer of psychiatric variables to route safety constraints without clear scientific/ethical validation. Route physiology and suit demand must preserve independent evidence limitations.
