# Temporal, individualized and population model

Each observation must include human subject pseudonym, variable ID, UTC and mission sol, mission phase, value, unit, measurement method, biological sample time, device calibration, quality flag, consent scope, and covariance where applicable.

Model separable time scales: seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, years. At each scale record both reversible adaptations and potential cumulative exposure.

Individual baselines must not be replaced by a generic "astronaut average" when interpreting clinical changes. Covariates include body size, sex-related physiology where medically relevant and consented, prior fitness, medical history, age, medications, sleep and task exposure. Protect sensitive information by design.

Distinguish observable signals (heart rate), inferred variables (autonomic tone), task performance (reaction time) and patient-reported experience (stress, pain). A missing blood specimen cannot be imputed from a wrist sensor.
