# Video, documentary and expedition research index

**Official publicly accessible research/video entrypoints**
- NASA HRP video collection, five hazards and short health explainers: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hrp-videos/
- NASA CHAPEA Mission 1 page with egress livestream/video material: https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/chapea/chapea-mission-1/
- NASA CHAPEA Mission 2 crew post-mission report (November 9, 2026): https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/johnson/chapea-mission-2-crew-reflects-after-378-days-on-simulated-mars/
- NASA Apollo 17 surface mission source and historical mission context: https://www.nasa.gov/missions/apollo/apollo-17-mission-details/
- ESA Mars500 study overview: https://www.esa.int/articles/mars500-study-overview

**Interpretation rule:** first-person astronaut recollections, documentaries and interviews provide qualitative context (perceived exertion, confinement, fatigue and decision making). They are not interchangeable with calibrated biomedical measurements. Record date, mission, speaker, original production/source, environment and exact claim when a video is later used to justify a variable.

**Review status:** links identified; individual full recordings have not been watched and time-coded in this registry. Do not invent quotes or claim a documentary establishes a clinical parameter.
