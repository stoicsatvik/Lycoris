# Reading priority: credible human spaceflight references

**Priority 1: official NASA standards and technical evidence**
1. NASA-STD-3001 Vol. 1 (Crew Health; 2023 edition): https://www.nasa.gov/reference/nasa-std-3001v1/
2. NASA-STD-3001 Vol. 2 (Human Factors, Habitability and Environmental Health; 2026 edition): https://www.nasa.gov/reference/nasa-std-3001v2/
3. Human Integration Design Handbook Rev. 1; human history, anthropometry, biomechanics and design rationale: https://www.nasa.gov/human-integration-design-handbook/
4. NASA Human Research Roadmap: https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/default.aspx and https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/evidence/
5. NASA Fundamentals of Human Health technical briefs: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/fundamentals-of-human-health/

**Priority 2: observed and analog programs**
6. NASA Twins Study summary (single human spaceflight case): https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/nasas-twins-study-results-published-in-science-journal/
7. NASA Apollo 17 mission operations: https://www.nasa.gov/missions/apollo/apollo-17-mission-details/
8. NASA CHAPEA analogs: https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/chapea/about-chapea/
9. ESA Mars500 520-day analog: https://www.esa.int/articles/mars500-study-overview

**Priority 3: computational frameworks and debate**
10. NASA Digital Astronaut Simulation: https://www.nasa.gov/general/digital-astronaut-simulation-tool/
11. NASA Integrated Medical Model (2026): https://www.nasa.gov/general/nasas-integrated-medical-model-imm/
12. NASA Glenn computational modeling: https://www.nasa.gov/glenn/glenn-expertise-space-exploration/human-health-performance/computational-modeling/
13. NASA NTRS 2021 partial-gravity health argument: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210019591 (hypothesis/inference, not human 0.38g proof)

No copyrighted book, manuscript, or video is reproduced here. These are source indexes, not claims that every linked document has undergone exhaustive extraction or independently verified every proposed metric.
