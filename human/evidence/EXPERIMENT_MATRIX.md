# Research regimes and directness

| Research regime | What can be learned | Critical limitation |
|---|---|---|
| ISS/microgravity human missions | Fluid shifts, cardiovascular changes, SANS, sleep, exercise, immune and endocrine adaptations | 0g and ISS conditions, not Mars 0.38g |
| Apollo lunar surface missions | Real human surface operations, maneuvering and task performance at ~0.16g | Short missions, different gravity, hardware and environment |
| NASA CHAPEA (378-day simulated Mars missions) | Isolation, resource constraints, communication delays, simulated EVA tasks, crew routines | Earth gravity, no real Mars radiation/dust/vacuum exposure |
| ESA Mars500 (520-day isolation) | Teamwork, circadian schedules, psychosocial response, delayed communication | Earth laboratory, no Mars physical environment |
| Bed-rest/head-down tilt | Partial analogue of unloading and cephalad fluid shift | Bed rest not actual spaceflight |
| Parabolic flight/harness gravity offload | Short-term reduced-gravity biomechanics | Seconds to minutes, incomplete mechanical equivalence |
| NASA Digital Astronaut Simulation | Musculoskeletal load and task feasibility under stated modeling choices | Computation must be validated against appropriate observations |
| Terrestrial clinical/exercise labs | Cardiorespiratory, hematologic, neurological and clinical relationships | Mars transfer functions often unknown |

No Mars mission crew cohort exists. Evidence for a mission must be explicitly tagged as direct-within-scope, analog-transfer, mechanistic hypothesis, or unsupported.
