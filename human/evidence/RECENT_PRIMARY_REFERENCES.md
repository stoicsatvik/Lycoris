# Additional primary NASA references checked November 14, 2026

The following public documents clarify **risk families, standards and research gaps**. They support researching categories, not assigning numerical values or calibrated formulas to every registered variable.

- NASA Integrated Medical Model overview: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-integrated-medical-model-imm-overview/ — NASA states a stochastic medical risk model using mission profiles, historical event incidence and resource use; standard assumptions may be optimistic. **Do not claim access to its private clinical database or runnable model.**
- NASA Integrated Medical Model September 16, 2026: https://www.nasa.gov/general/nasas-integrated-medical-model-imm/ — describes modeling **more than 100** medical events, mission time lost and other outcomes. No medical probability values imported.
- NASA Integrated Medical Model documentation and medical-condition cases: https://www.nasa.gov/integrated-medical-model-documentation/
- NASA-STD-3001 Volume 1 Rev C, September 15, 2023: https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-STD-3001_VOL_1 — crew health requirements, not a universal physiological parameter dataset.
- NASA-STD-3001 Volume 2 Rev F, July 14, 2026: https://standards.nasa.gov/node/237 — human factors, habitability and environmental design requirements.
- NASA risk of inadequate food and nutrition, updated August 27, 2025: https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/esdmd/hhp/food-and-nutrition-risk/ — food safety, intake, nutritional adequacy and mission duration.
- NASA HRR host-microorganism interactions: https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Risks/risk.aspx?i=80 — microbiome/pathogen and immunity risks, with mission-specific risk dispositions.
- NASA HRR integrated diet–immune–microbiota research: https://humanresearchroadmap.nasa.gov/Tasks/task.aspx?i=2068 — interrelated nutrition and immune-microbiome adaptation.
- NASA radiation-induced cancer risk: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/risk-of-radiation-induced-cancers/ — possible long-term cancer and degenerative effects; no exact individual Mars risk probability.
- NASA Digital Astronaut Simulation Tool: https://www.nasa.gov/general/digital-astronaut-simulation-tool/ — reduced-gravity biomechanics and models using motion capture and OpenSim.
- NASA HRP medical/behavioral risk catalog: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/ — altered immunity, sensorimotor, cardiovascular and operational hazards.
- NASA crew health record security: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/7-0-crew-health-records/ — secure handling of crew clinical data.

**Reading status:** page-level sources and summaries reviewed, not an exhaustive systematic review or extraction of every article, standard PDF, book, instrument manual or documentary. Full citation-level audits and model calibration remain future research.
