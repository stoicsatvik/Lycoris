# Missing evidence and non-identifiability

No human population has completed long-duration residence in actual Mars surface gravity. No directly validated universal transformation exists from 1g, 0g or 0.16g human physiology to 0.38g across every organ system.

Particularly limited:
1. Long-term musculoskeletal and cardiovascular response trajectories at partial Mars gravity.
2. Individual physiology under Martian EVA while carrying a particular suit's inertial mass and resistive joint mechanics.
3. Reliable per-person radiation-induced health risk from cosmic particle spectra, duration and tissue doses.
4. Individualized psychiatric disorder predictions from isolation conditions or wearable biometrics.
5. Microbial evolution, immune response and medication efficacy over years in distant habitats.
6. Site-specific dust toxicology and chronic human exposure pathways.
7. Emergency medicine under delays, limited supplies and unknown operational medicine performance.

When these factors matter, report hypothesis, empirical analogue or unknown with conservative uncertainty. Do not manufacture 1,691 interconnected quantitative coefficients; most are not identifiable from available evidence.
