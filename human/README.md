# Human systems: anatomy, physiology, psychology and adaptation

Purpose: a **human-only** evidence and parameter registry for hypothetical Mars surface operations and the interplanetary journey. No spacesuit design or medical clearance model lives here.

## Scope
- `parameters/anatomy/` — body structures, organ systems, and measurable regional characteristics.
- `parameters/physiology/` — metabolic, cardiovascular, pulmonary, blood, renal, immune, neuromuscular and other physiological functions.
- `parameters/neuroscience/` — vision, vestibular, sensory and central-nervous-system function.
- `parameters/psychology/` — cognition, mental health, sleep, psychosocial adaptation and team dynamics.
- `parameters/space_exposure/` — gravity transitions, radiation, confinement, atmosphere, toxic exposures and latency.
- `parameters/operations/` — physical work, fatigue, injuries, clinical response and recovery.
- `parameters/` — machine-readable, unpopulated **candidate human variables**. Parameter IDs are Lycoris conventions, not official NASA telemetry channels.
- `evidence/` — named primary research sources, historical flight/analog limits, reading and video lists.
- `models/` — state transitions, cause/evidence graph, uncertainty, identifiability and eventual suit-boundary contract.
- `validation/` — tests and falsification requirements.

## Important scientific boundaries
1. No human has walked on Mars. Mars-specific physiology cannot be labeled directly observed.
2. Apollo lunar (0.16g), low Earth orbit (microgravity), terrestrial analogs (1g), and Martian surface (about 0.38g) are distinct evidence regimes.
3. Published NASA standards establish requirements, **not measured values for a hypothetical individual**.
4. Human physiology is person- and context-dependent. No invented universal normal, pathology cutoff, toxicology tolerance, 0.38g adaptation coefficient, or psychiatric prediction is a valid model parameter.
5. A catalog of possible measures is not a catalog of existing astronaut datasets. Sensitive information requires access controls, consent and aggregate presentation by default.
6. Neither MRI, blood tests nor psychometrics are automatically accessible from a suit.
7. Do not mix human metabolic oxygen *demand* with suit oxygen *supply*. The suit is a separate future subsystem.

**Status:** schema and research declarations only. No private crew medical records, real biomedical measurements, Mars physiological response coefficients or calibrated clinical risk model have been added.
