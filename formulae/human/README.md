# Astronaut physiology and locomotion

Responsibilities: walking pace, workload, oxygen metabolic demand, CO2 and heat generation, rest periods, fatigue and performance variation.

Inputs: astronaut profile, mass and carried load, movement segments, time and suit constraints. Outputs: time-indexed physiological demands with assumptions and uncertainty.

NASA/Apollo/ICES research may inform scenarios, but no unsupported transfer of lunar/Earth performance data is equivalent to validated human Marswalking data.
