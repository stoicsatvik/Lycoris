# Bayesian probability

State the unknown parameter, prior distribution, observation model, likelihood, and the evidence used for updating. Evaluate the posterior and posterior predictive quantities only where these elements are defensible.

Distinguish prior assumptions from measured data. Handle correlated measurement errors and evaluate sensitivity to prior choices. Historical Curiosity/Gale data do not magically become local Jezero measurements through a probability calculation.

When evidence is insufficient, report broad conditional estimates or abstain instead of fabricating numerical certainty.
