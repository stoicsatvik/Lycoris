# Fermi estimates and independent bounds

Decompose an unfamiliar quantity into measurable or assumed primitive factors. Use honest order-of-magnitude ranges and dimensional analysis to check detailed outputs.

Examples: travel time = distance / speed under a specified speed range; minimum route length must respect geometric bounds; total consumables must be at least physically modeled consumption when losses and reserves are nonnegative.

Fermi estimates are plausibility screens, **not** probability distributions or field evidence. Record each assumption and sensitivity.
